🔴Beginners Guide to Dodgeball



    🔴 Basic Rules

  • Players start behind the baseline
  • Rush to grab balls at the center line
  • Hit opponents below the shoulders to get them out
  • Catch a ball to eliminate the thrower
  • Step out of bounds = out

    🎽Dodgeball Equipment

  • Soft foam or rubber balls
  • Court with center and attack lines
  • Team uniforms
  • Boundary markers

    ⚡Basic Techniques

  • Quick dodging and side-stepping
  • Aiming low for accurate throws
  • Catching safely to bring teammates back
  • Coordinated team attacks
  • Staying alert and moving constantly
